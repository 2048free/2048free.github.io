Flappy 2048

Flappy 2048 is a single-player arcade game that crosses Flappy Bird with 2048. You steer a number tile through gaps in a stream of green walls, and every five walls you pass, the tile merges up to the next number, from 2 all the way to 2048.

How to play Flappy 2048

Tap or click the board, or press Space, ↑ or W, to flap. Let go and you fall. Each wall you pass scores a point, and every five walls your tile doubles, so 2048 takes 50 walls. Touch a wall and the run is over, but the floor and ceiling are safe, so you can rest on the floor when the next gap is low. Only the middle 45% of your tile counts as a hit, so near misses are forgiven. The game pauses on its own if you scroll away or switch tabs, and gives you a second of safety when you come back.

What the numbers say

The walls start out scrolling at 2 cells per second and speed up a little with every wall. By the 50th wall they are 62% faster, and the time between walls has shrunk from 1.7 seconds to about 1 second. A flawless run to 2048 takes about 67 seconds.

Every gap is two cells tall and can only sit in three places: top, middle, or bottom. So the next gap is at the same height a third of the time, one step away 44% of the time, and a full top-to-bottom swing about 1 time in 5.

Tips

  • Tap in a steady rhythm instead of mashing. One flap lifts the tile only about 0.6 of a cell.
  • Climbs are harder than drops. Gravity does the work going down, but getting from a bottom gap to a top gap takes several flaps, so start early.
  • Use the floor. If the next gap is at the bottom, there's no need to hover.

FAQs

Why did the game pause by itself?

It pauses when less than half of the board is on screen or the tab is hidden, so you never crash while reading. Tap or press Space to carry on. The walls can't hurt you for the first second.

What happens after I reach 2048?

You win, and you can keep flying. The walls keep getting faster until they top out at 3.6 cells per second after 64 walls.

Is my best score saved?

Yes. Best is the most walls you've passed in one run. It's saved in your browser on this device, separately from the other games on this site.
