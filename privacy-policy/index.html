Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

This policy explains what information 2048free ("we", "the site") collects when you visit https://2048free.github.io.

Information stored on your device

The games save your best score and your light/dark theme choice in your browser's local storage. This data stays on your device and is never sent to us. You can remove it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages are visited and roughly where visitors come from. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information like your IP address (anonymized), browser type, device, and pages viewed. This information is processed by Google according to the Google Privacy Policy. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses for security purposes; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children's privacy

The site does not knowingly collect personal information from children and has no accounts, forms, or sign-ups.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page.
